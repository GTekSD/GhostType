# GhostType - GTekSD Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

GhostType helps you enter text into a webpage field that you select. This policy explains what the extension handles and how it is used.

## Information handled

GhostType handles:

- Text snippets that you enter or choose to save in the extension.
- Clipboard text only when you select **Paste**.
- The focused webpage field needed to insert text after you select **Start**.
- Extension preferences such as typing speed and theme.

This information is processed locally by the extension in your browser. Text snippets and preferences may be stored in the extension's local browser storage so they remain available when you reopen it. Clipboard text is read only following your Paste action.

When you start typing, the text you selected is inserted into the webpage field you chose. That website may receive and process the text according to its own privacy policy.

## Sharing and transmission

GhostType does not send snippets, clipboard contents, webpage field contents, or browsing history to GhostType/GTekSD servers. The extension does not include analytics, advertising, or an account service.

## Retention and deletion

Saved snippets and preferences remain in local extension storage until you clear them or remove the extension. You can clear a snippet from its field in the side panel. Removing the extension removes its locally stored data according to your browser's behavior.

## Permissions

GhostType uses browser permissions to show its side panel, read the clipboard after you select Paste, and deliver your typing command to the webpage where you choose to type. Website compatibility varies; pages may ignore synthetic input events.
